Governed autonomy

Scale agency without surrendering authority.

Every agent is an identity operating inside a bounded environment. Define where it may work, what it may see, what it may spend, and which decisions must remain human.

Identity
Role-based access

Separate organizations, workspaces, teams, agents, data, credentials, tools, and tenant boundaries.

Control
Human approval gates

Require explicit authorization before consequential changes, submissions, payments, deployments, or external communications.

Evidence
Immutable activity history

Preserve commands, tool calls, files, browser activity, state transitions, costs, decisions, and artifacts.

Deployment
Cloud, VPC, or private environment

Place execution where institutional policy and data sovereignty require it to live.

Limits
Budgets and policy ceilings

Apply financial, temporal, operational, and tool-specific limits to every mission.

Recovery
Checkpoint, replay, and escalation

Resume safely, reproduce the execution path, and hand authority to a person when confidence falls.
