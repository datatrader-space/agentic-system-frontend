FAQ

Common questions.

Short answers on plans, metering, and deployment. For anything specific to your institution, talk to the North Rays team.

Is there really a free tier?

Yes. Developer is free and includes a real workspace, browser and Daytona sandboxes, and a monthly execution allowance—enough to ship a first governed workflow.

How does metering work?

Execution is billed on model tokens, active workspace minutes, and tool calls. Every mission and organization has budgets and ceilings you set in advance.

Can I cap spend?

Per-mission and per-org budgets, per-tool limits, and read-only modes prevent runaway cost. Work pauses or escalates instead of exceeding a ceiling.

Can we deploy in our own environment?

Enterprise supports dedicated environments and VPC. Sovereign adds on-premise, air-gapped runners, data residency, and retention controls.

Do you offer annual billing?

Team and above can be billed annually with volume and commitment discounts. Talk to sales for an institutional agreement.

What support is included?

Community for Developer, priority email for Team, SLA-backed support for Enterprise, and a dedicated team for Sovereign deployments.
