Retries and recovery

Retry individual nodes with configurable backoff, route exhausted runs to a dead-letter queue, and replay from a safe checkpoint.

  • Per-node timeout and retry policy
  • Replay with original or edited inputs
  • Compensation paths for partial failure

  • Schema-aware variables
  • Transforms and validation
  • Secrets remain masked in previews and logs
